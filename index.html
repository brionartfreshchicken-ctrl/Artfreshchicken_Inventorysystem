Full System Backup
Everything, in one file
One file with every report and record in the system, each as its own sheet (or its own labeled section if saved as CSV): Summary, Transactions (one row per completed sale — subtotal, discount, total, payment method), Sales Detail (every individual line, not added together), Best Sellers, Waste & Losses, the full Movement Log, current Inventory, the Restock List, Suppliers, Purchases (every purchase, one row per line item), Recipes, Production (every batch, one row per ingredient used), Operating Expenses, Staff Directory, Man Power (labor cost, Gross Profit after paying it, and real Net Profit after Operating Expenses), LPG Usage, Product / Food Costing Profitability, and Food Costing Detail (every ingredient in every food, not summed into a total). Account passwords are never included.
